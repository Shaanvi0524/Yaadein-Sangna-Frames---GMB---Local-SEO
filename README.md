Google Business Profile Optimization for Yaadein / Sangna Frame

Project Overview

This project focuses on optimizing the Google Business Profile of Yaadein / Sangna Frame, a photo frame manufacturing business located in Morbi, Gujarat. The objective is to improve local search visibility, present accurate business information, and help potential customers discover the business online.

Project Objectives

* Review and optimize the Google Business Profile.
* Research relevant local SEO keywords.
* Improve the business description and product information.
* Review business information consistency across online listings.
* Analyze Google Business Profile performance.
* Identify opportunities for further local SEO improvement.

Work Completed

* Reviewed the Google Business Profile information.
* Updated the business description to include relevant local keywords naturally.
* Checked the primary business category.
* Reviewed product names, descriptions, and photos.
* Published Google Business Profile posts featuring products.
* Reviewed the IndiaMART business listing.
* Recorded profile performance metrics.
* Tested relevant Google search queries.

Keyword Research

The following keywords were considered relevant to the business:

* Photo frame manufacturer in Morbi
* Photo frame manufacturers in Morbi Gujarat
* Frame manufacturer in Morbi
* Photo frame supplier in Morbi
* Folding photo frame
* Multi-photo frame

Search Visibility Observations

During manual Google searches, Yaadein / Sangna Frame appeared for “frame manufacturer in Morbi.” It was not visible in the observed results for “photo frame manufacturer in Morbi” and “photo frame manufacturers in Morbi Gujarat.”

These observations represent searches performed during the project. Search results may vary by location, time, and search context. No specific ranking improvement is claimed.

Challenges Identified

* The business was not visible in every target keyword search tested.
* No website clicks were recorded, and a website link was not available on the profile.
* Calls, chat clicks, and bookings were recorded as zero for the selected reporting period.
* Further monitoring is required to assess the impact of optimization activities.

Recommendations

* Keep business information accurate and up to date.
* Continue adding genuine, high-quality product photos and relevant updates.
* Review search visibility periodically using consistent keywords and search conditions.
* Maintain accurate business details across online listings.
* Consider developing a business website or product catalogue in the future.
* Monitor performance metrics over time and compare equivalent reporting periods.

Conclusion

This project provided practical experience in Google Business Profile management, local SEO keyword research, business listing review, product presentation, and performance analysis. The findings identify opportunities for Yaadein / Sangna Frame to improve online discoverability and customer engagement. Continued monitoring will be needed to evaluate future results.
